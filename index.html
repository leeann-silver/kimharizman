Coverage

Areas Served

Westport, CT

Westport is one of Connecticut's most sought-after communities, known for its excellent schools, vibrant downtown, and proximity to New York City. The luxury market features waterfront estates on Long Island Sound, historic homes near downtown, and new construction throughout.

  • Compo Beach waterfront
  • Saugatuck neighborhood
  • Downtown walkability
  • Top-ranked schools
  • 60-minute Metro-North to NYC

Weston, CT

Weston offers a rural, equestrian-friendly lifestyle with large estate properties set on multi-acre lots. Known for its privacy and natural beauty, Weston attracts buyers seeking space and serenity within commuting distance of Manhattan.

  • Estate properties on 2-5+ acres
  • Equestrian-friendly zoning
  • Devil's Den Nature Preserve
  • Highly rated Weston Public Schools

Fairfield, CT

Fairfield blends New England charm with beach town appeal. Fairfield Beach and Penfield Beach neighborhoods command premium prices, while downtown Fairfield offers walkable dining and shopping.

  • Fairfield Beach & Penfield Beach
  • Fairfield University area
  • Historic town center
  • Strong rental and investment market

Wilton, CT

Wilton is a prestigious residential community with top-performing schools and a family-oriented lifestyle. Large lots and wooded settings define Wilton's luxury market.

  • Top 10 CT school district
  • Large-lot zoning
  • Wilton Center shops & dining
  • Proximity to Norwalk and Stamford

Norwalk, CT

Norwalk offers waterfront living along the Long Island Sound with a growing arts and dining scene. South Norwalk (SoNo) has emerged as a vibrant neighborhood attracting young professionals and investors.

  • SoNo waterfront district
  • Rowayton village
  • Investment and condo opportunities
  • I-95 and Metro-North access
